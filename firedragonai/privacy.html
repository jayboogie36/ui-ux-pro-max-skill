PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Fire Dragon AI ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website or become a client, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email info@firedragonai.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone, business details, and any logos, photos, or files you upload during onboarding or via our forms.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your full card details.
  • Usage data — pages visited, clicks, device and browser info, collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools.

2. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies — including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics — to understand traffic, measure ads, and improve our services. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google opt-out add-on, and manage ad preferences in your Meta and Google account settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To build, deliver, and support the services you request
  • To process payments and send confirmations, contracts, and updates
  • To run and measure marketing (including ads and retargeting)
  • To respond to your questions and improve our site and services

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, such as: Stripe (payments), GoHighLevel (CRM, forms, email), Meta and Google (advertising & analytics), and our website hosting provider — each under their own terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Call recording (AI voice service)

If you are a client using our AI voice answering service, calls may be recorded and/or transcribed. The service announces recording at the start of each call to comply with Illinois all-party-consent law.

6. Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails (use the unsubscribe link or email us). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

10. Contact
